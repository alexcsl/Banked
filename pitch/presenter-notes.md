# Banked pitch and demo

## 45-second pitch

"I know what it feels like when trade size grows faster than discipline. Banked lets me set how much SOL stays in my wallet. With a 10 SOL target, a 9 SOL wallet balance sells nothing; an 11 SOL balance makes 1 SOL eligible. In our simulation, the resulting USDC is budgeted 20% for SPYx, 20% for JUP, and 60% to keep. The rule starts paused, and each live sale and purchase needs my approval. This is a simulation today; live wallet trading still needs validation."

## Live demo sequence

1. Explain that the 10 SOL starting position is illustrative. Show the 20/20/60 rule and record the simulated 1 SOL sale.
2. Point to 125.030864 USDC in proceeds, 50.012344 awaiting purchases, and 75.01852 planned to remain.
3. Record the SPYx purchase. Point to 25.006172 routed to SPYx and 25.006172 still awaiting JUP.
4. Refresh the page, resume the operation, and complete JUP.
5. Show the updated dashboard and saved receipt.

Describe dashboard amounts as saved operation records. They are not a live wallet balance or evidence of mainnet trading.

Banked does not calculate cost basis or automatically identify a 1 SOL gain. The trader sets the SOL balance to keep trading with, and Banked proposes selling only the wallet balance above it. A rule starts paused and every live sale still needs a signature. The historical Barber and Odean study on slide 1 concerns US stock brokerage accounts from 1991 to 1996; it supports the risk of excessive trading, not a claim that Banked improves returns. Source: https://faculty.haas.berkeley.edu/odean/Papers%20current%20versions/Individual_Investor_Performance_Final.pdf

The Solana Foundation reports that xStocks launched on Solana in June 2025. That supports the timing and same-chain route on slide 6, subject to eligibility and product access. Source: https://solana.com/id/news/case-study-xstocks

For wallet questions: an in-app sale is associated with the connected wallet and the rule selected before selling. Sales made in another app do not trigger Banked automatically. The user can scan recent wallet exits, review a candidate, and apply the rule afterward. Each follow-up purchase requires a separate wallet signature. Live trading is still gated pending transaction inspection and eligibility checks.
